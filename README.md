Vice Cover Studio — turn your photo and a caption into a multi-panel GTA 6-style poster with a Vice City sunset grade, then export it as a PNG.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Load a photo into each of the three panels (or one photo in all three), type a title and tagline, pick a layout and grade, frame each panel with zoom and pan, choose 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080, and hit Export PNG. Everything runs offline from the files under `app/` — photos never leave your machine, and the sun, palms and lettering are drawn by the app itself.
